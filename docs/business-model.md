# Business Model

_Summarized from the team's Business Model Canvas (YUKTI Proto ID
IR2025-947774) and pitch deck. This is a reference for the business plan —
it doesn't describe anything implemented in this repo's code._

## Value proposition

Track movement, disturbances, restlessness, and sleep duration without a
worn device; convert that into clear daily/weekly sleep insights, with
optional heart-rate and snore analysis.

## Customer segments

- Students (18-25) with irregular sleep schedules
- Working professionals (22-45) with high stress and screen exposure
- Shift workers with rotating schedules
- Fitness/wellness-focused individuals
- Parents monitoring children's sleep
- Clinics and wellness centers (multi-user deployments)

## Channels

Mobile app (Android/iOS) as primary access point, product website, social
media/digital campaigns, clinic/wellness-center partnerships, word of mouth.

## Revenue streams

- One-time hardware sales
- Monthly subscription for insights (₹59-99)
- Cloud data plans
- B2B partnerships with clinics/wellness centers
- White-label licensing
- API access for partners (usage-based or license fee)

## Cost structure

Hardware components (ESP32, MPU6050, battery), manufacturing & assembly,
app development & maintenance, cloud storage & analytics, marketing &
distribution.

## Key partners

Component/sensor manufacturers (MPU6050, ESP32, MAX30102), PCB
manufacturers, 3D printing vendors, mobile app developers, wellness clinics,
distribution partners.

## Market sizing

- **TAM**: Global sleep tech & wellness market, $80B+
- **SAM**: Digital sleep apps & consumer wearables, ~$25B
- **Target market**: India & APAC students/professionals, est. $1.5-2B

## Competitive positioning

- AI-driven coaching (not just raw tracking) vs. ~70% of competitors that
  only give generic tips
- Designed specifically for irregular sleepers (students, shift workers)
- Priced well below premium sleep rings/wearables (₹20,000-₹40,000)
- Full data-pipeline control from sensor to AI analysis

## IP strategy

Per the pitch deck: software copyright on core AI logic, trade-secret
protection for the sleep-scoring/prompt-engineering workflows, with future
patent filings planned for the sleep analysis and recommendation
methodology.
